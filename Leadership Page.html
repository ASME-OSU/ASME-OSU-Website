Officer roles & responsibilities

How each officer helps our chapter run.

President

Leads chapter meetings, coordinates the officer team, and appoints committees with the board’s approval.

Vice President / Corporate Liaison

Supports the President and builds industry partnerships, bringing in sponsors and recognizing their support for the chapter.

Treasurer

Keeps chapter finances organized, from advisor-approved payments and purchase orders to sponsor invoices and financial records.

Administrator

Coordinates meeting spaces and chapter merchandise, making sure rooms are reserved and orders reach members.

Social Chair

Brings members together through social events, coordinating plans, promotion, and budgets with the President and Treasurer.

Webmaster

Maintains the chapter website and social accounts so members can find events, officer contacts, and current information.

E-Council Representative

Connects ASME with Engineering Council, shares meeting updates, maintains chapter registration, and coordinates funding requests with the Treasurer.
