Part 01

Privacy Policy

Information practices for the chapter website and the activities described here.

Who this page covers

“ASME OSU,” “the chapter,” “we,” and “our” refer to the American Society of Mechanical Engineers student chapter at The Ohio State University. This page covers our chapter website, its features, and the chapter information practices described below. The chapter is a student organization; registration does not make it a legal entity of Ohio State. Chapter officers cannot bind the university or ASME International through this page.

Ohio State’s hosting and university services are also subject to the University Privacy Statement and University Cookie Statement. ASME International, employers, social platforms, and other providers operate their own services under their own notices. This chapter policy does not replace those notices.

Information you provide

  • Newsletter signup: first and last name, email address, graduation year, and optional OSU Name.#. The Join form submits these fields to Brevo, our newsletter provider.
  • Chapter participation: event registrations, check-ins, public display names, activity participation, points, and related corrections you submit through chapter forms.
  • Messages: contact information and message contents you send about membership, sponsorship, accessibility, privacy, or other chapter matters.
  • Career resources: résumés and accompanying information you choose to submit for the chapter résumé book or recruiting activities.
  • Public profiles and media: officer information, photographs, and other content provided for chapter publication.

Provide accurate information and only information you are entitled to share. Do not submit passwords, government identification numbers, payment-card details, health records, or private information about another person through ordinary chapter forms or Gearly. If an activity needs additional information, follow that activity’s specific instructions.

Technical information, cookies, and analytics

When you visit, hosting, security, analytics, and content-delivery providers may receive your IP address, device and browser details, requested files, referring pages, timestamps, and interaction information. Ohio State’s CampusPress/WordPress hosts the website; GitHub Pages delivers custom assets and selected public chapter feeds. External requests disclose technical connection information to the receiving provider even when you do not submit a form.

Cookies and similar technologies may support login, security, site operation, preferences, and usage measurement. The website loads Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager integrations for usage measurement and tag delivery. Which tags run and what they collect depend on administrator settings, provider behavior, and your choices. See Ohio State’s Cookie Statement and use any cookie controls presented by the university, together with your browser settings. A cookie setting may not clear local or session storage.

Gearly and guided navigation use browser storage for conversation state, navigation progress, and preferences. The signup celebration also uses temporary browser state. Gearly currently answers from prepared chapter content and public sources in your browser; it does not send questions to a generative AI service. Clearing site data can reset these features. Browser-local information may remain accessible to other people using the same browser profile.

Why we use information

We use relevant information to send chapter announcements, coordinate events and membership activities, maintain participation and points records, answer requests, support recruiting activities you select, manage sponsorship inquiries, publish approved chapter content, troubleshoot features, and understand service use. Technical information may also help providers prevent abuse and keep services functioning.

Information supplied for a particular activity is subject to that activity’s stated purpose and any additional notice. Newsletter signup alone does not register you for an event, authorize distribution of a résumé, or enroll you in ASME International membership. Where applicable law requires a particular legal basis or consent, that requirement still applies; this page does not substitute for obtaining it.

Newsletter delivery and tracking

Brevo processes signup and subscription information and may record confirmation, delivery, bounce, unsubscribe, and engagement events. Depending on campaign settings, emails may contain tracking pixels and individualized links that measure opens and clicks and may associate those events with a subscriber. Email privacy tools and automated scanners can affect those measurements. See Brevo’s privacy policy.

Use a newsletter’s unsubscribe link or contact asme@osu.edu to stop chapter newsletter delivery. Unsubscribing does not automatically delete attendance records, submitted résumés, prior correspondence, or provider suppression records used to honor an opt-out. Browser cookie controls do not necessarily disable tracking in an email client.

Who may receive information

  • Chapter administration: officers and people assisting with the relevant chapter activity.
  • Service providers: hosting, email, forms, storage, analytics, and other tools used to operate the chapter’s services. Providers’ access, storage locations, retention, and safeguards depend on their services and terms.
  • Recruiting recipients: participating sponsors and recruiting partners receive the chapter résumé book when you submit a résumé for that purpose. A recipient may retain a copy and contact you under its own practices.
  • Activity organizers: information needed for an event or opportunity may be shared as described in that activity’s registration or submission materials.
  • Legal and safety needs: information may be disclosed where required by law or valid legal process, or as lawfully necessary to address security incidents, protect safety, or resolve a dispute.

Submitting a sponsorship inquiry does not authorize a sponsor to receive private membership rosters. Public recognition of sponsors does not itself give them access to private chapter records. Review separate forms before choosing to share information.

Leaderboards, photos, and public content

The website’s member leaderboard uses selected fields from an approved public export, such as the public name you choose, points, and rankings. Private rosters, personal email identifiers, and individual attendance records are not part of that public website export. A chosen display name may still identify you; “public” does not mean anonymous.

Officer profiles, chapter news, event photographs, and gallery content from chapter Instagram and Google Photos sources may appear publicly. Visitors, search engines, and other services may copy or archive public material. Removing a chapter copy cannot guarantee removal from someone else’s device, a search cache, or a third-party platform.

Contact us about a photo, profile, or display name you want corrected or removed. Include the page URL and identify the relevant item. Attending an event or visiting this website is not, by itself, a blanket release of your image or ownership rights. Any activity-specific media permission is handled separately.

External services and international processing

Links and features may involve Google Calendar, Google Forms or Sheets, Google Photos, Brevo, GitHub, GroupMe, Instagram, LinkedIn, ASME International, employers, or other services. Following a link, joining a group, submitting an external form, or loading hosted content may cause the provider to receive information under its own policies. Joining GroupMe can expose your profile or messages to other group participants according to that service’s settings.

Providers and recipients may process information outside your state or country, where privacy rules may differ. This page does not promise a particular storage location or international-transfer mechanism. Review the provider’s current notices before sharing information. Chapter officers cannot control another organization’s independent processing.

Retention, deletion, and security

Retention varies with the record’s purpose, membership and activity administration, correction or dispute needs, provider settings, backups, and applicable legal or university requirements. We do not promise one retention period for every record. Contact the chapter for information about a particular record. Information subject to a retention obligation or a legal hold may need to be kept after an activity ends.

Deletion from an active chapter system may not immediately erase backups, email recipients’ copies, provider logs, public caches, or records held independently by recruiting partners. Provider suppression records may be retained to prevent renewed mailings. Requests will be assessed against the records and requirements involved.

No service can guarantee complete security or uninterrupted availability. Use a secure device and connection, protect your accounts, and report suspected unauthorized access or disclosure promptly. We will assess reported incidents and follow applicable notification obligations; this page does not promise that every incident will be discovered or reported on a fixed schedule.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to submit optional information, unsubscribe from newsletters, leave external groups, adjust provider account settings, and clear browser cookies and storage. Some choices may limit a feature or prevent registration for an activity that needs the information.

Email asme@osu.edu to request access to, correction of, or deletion of chapter-held information, or to raise a privacy concern. Describe the record and request without sending sensitive identity documents. We may request reasonable information to confirm identity and authority before disclosing or changing a record. If another organization controls the information, we may direct you to that organization.

Additional rights, such as objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to a regulator, may be available where applicable law provides them. Scope, exceptions, and deadlines depend on the law and the responsible organization. This notice does not waive rights or claim that every privacy law applies to every chapter activity. University-controlled records may be subject to university retention and public-records requirements.

Minors and youth activities

The website is intended primarily for university students and the chapter’s wider engineering community. The general newsletter and website guide are not intended to collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted information should contact the chapter so the situation can be reviewed and addressed under applicable requirements.

Youth outreach, travel, and other activities involving minors may require separate registration, parent or guardian permission, and university procedures. This notice is not parental consent, a youth-program authorization, or an age-verification system.

Conditions for using chapter website features and understanding related activities.

Scope and relationship to other rules

If you cannot follow these conditions, do not use the affected feature. A transaction or activity requiring affirmative consent must use its own appropriate acceptance process; merely viewing this page does not sign a waiver or separate contract.

This page does not amend a signed agreement, create an insurance policy, establish university representation, or override rights that cannot lawfully be limited. Acceptable use and accurate submissions

Use chapter services respectfully and provide information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge. Submit your own registrations and check-ins unless authorized to act for someone else. You must have permission to submit another person’s content or information.

Do not impersonate another person; falsify attendance, points, or credentials; send spam or malicious files; harass or threaten others; infringe intellectual-property or privacy rights; attempt unauthorized access; bypass access controls; harvest personal information; or overload or disrupt chapter systems. Automated access must respect access restrictions, applicable provider terms, and other people’s privacy. Public display of information does not authorize abusive collection or reuse.

We may remove inappropriate content or restrict an affected chapter feature to address misuse, security concerns, or applicable requirements, subject to university procedures and law. Website access restrictions do not themselves decide university disciplinary matters or chapter membership rights.

Membership, points, and benefits

The Join page connects you to the chapter newsletter and GroupMe. Those steps do not guarantee an event place, elected office, internship, job, scholarship, prize, or other benefit. ASME International registration, eligibility, dues, and benefits are separate.

Points, rankings, and public feeds may be delayed, incomplete, or affected by verification and technical errors. Officers may correct mistakes and review disputed entries under the published points rules. Contact the chapter with the event and supporting details for a correction. Published eligibility requirements and activity rules govern any benefit; a displayed rank or total alone is not a promise of an award or payment.

Events, safety, accessibility, and changes

Dates, venues, speakers, availability, capacity, and eligibility may change, and events may be postponed or cancelled. Check the latest organizer announcement before traveling or making arrangements. An RSVP may require a separate confirmation and does not guarantee admission where capacity or eligibility restrictions apply.

Follow organizer, venue, equipment, travel, and safety instructions and applicable university rules. Engineering demonstrations, workshops, travel, and physical activities may carry risks that depend on the activity. Ask organizers about requirements, supervision, accessibility, and any necessary accommodations before participating; do not undertake a task you cannot perform safely.

Activity-specific consent, safety training, travel arrangements, and any required waiver or permission must be handled separately. These website terms do not release injury claims or replace an appropriate event waiver. Contact the chapter as early as practical for accommodation requests. The chapter email is not an emergency service; contact emergency responders for urgent danger.

Fees, purchases, donations, and sponsorship

Joining the chapter newsletter and GroupMe is currently free. Optional events, trips, merchandise, national membership, or other activities may have separate charges. Review the stated price, eligibility, payment recipient, cancellation conditions, and refund policy before committing. If a policy is missing or unclear, contact the organizer before paying.

This page does not create a universal refund rule or authorize charges. A cancellation does not itself determine reimbursement of independent travel or third-party purchases. Applicable law and any valid transaction-specific agreement govern. Do not send payment-card details through ordinary chapter email or forms.

Sponsorship descriptions are informational. A submitted inquiry does not create a sponsorship contract; deliverables, payment, use of names or logos, résumé access, and any exclusivity require an appropriate written agreement. The chapter does not promise donations are tax deductible, and university registration alone does not establish tax-exempt status.

You may view and use website resources for lawful personal and educational purposes subject to any stated license. Public access does not transfer copyright or grant permission to use Ohio State, ASME, sponsor, or other marks as an endorsement. Seek the relevant owner’s permission for uses requiring it.

You retain ownership of material you submit. Provide only content you are entitled to share.
