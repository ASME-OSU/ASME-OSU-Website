About this policy

This policy describes the website and chapter activities of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers student chapter at The Ohio State University (ASME OSU). It does not describe every service offered by Ohio State or ASME International.

For university services, also see Ohio State’s University Privacy Statement and Cookie Statement. External websites and services have their own privacy practices.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter signup: first and last name, email address, graduation year, and an optional OSU Name.#. Our signup form sends these details to Brevo, our email service provider.
  • Participation and member points: information provided through chapter check-in and activity forms, including the public name you choose for the leaderboard and participation used to calculate points.
  • Messages and submissions: information you choose to send us, such as membership questions, sponsorship inquiries, or a résumé submitted for the chapter résumé book.
  • Website activity: hosting and external service providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and requested pages or files when your browser connects to them.

How information is used and shared

Chapter officers use information to manage membership communications, announce events and opportunities, maintain participation and points records, answer requests, and coordinate chapter activities. Providers such as Brevo process information to deliver the services we use.

If you submit a résumé for the chapter résumé book, it is intended to be shared with participating sponsors and recruiting partners, as described on the submission resource. Include only information you want those recipients to receive.

Information may also need to be retained or disclosed when required by applicable law or university requirements. Signing up for the chapter newsletter does not itself enroll you in ASME International membership.

What may appear publicly

The member leaderboard displays selected information from an approved public export, including members’ chosen public names, points, and rankings. Private rosters, personal email identifiers, and individual attendance records are not part of that public website export.

Officer profiles and chapter event photos may be published on the website. The gallery includes content from chapter Instagram and Google Photos sources. Public content can be viewed, copied, or indexed by others.

Contact asme@osu.edu to request a public-name correction or removal, or to raise a concern about a photo. Include the page link and enough detail to identify the content.

Website services and browser storage

This website runs on Ohio State’s CampusPress/WordPress service. Custom assets and public chapter data are delivered through GitHub Pages. Newsletter signup uses Brevo. Google services support chapter calendars, forms, and public data; social links connect to services including GroupMe, Instagram, and LinkedIn. Their privacy practices apply when you use those services.

The website uses browser storage for features such as Gearly’s conversation state, guided navigation, preferences, and the signup celebration flow. Gearly is a browser-based chapter guide using prepared answers and public chapter information; its current implementation does not send your questions to a generative AI service.

You can clear stored site data or restrict cookies and storage through your browser settings. Some preferences and guided features may reset or stop working. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into Gearly.

Keeping information

Retention depends on the type of record, the chapter activity, the provider holding it, and applicable university or legal requirements. Contact the chapter for information about a particular record or to request deletion. Unsubscribing stops newsletter delivery but does not necessarily erase every related record.

No online service can guarantee complete security. Please do not send passwords, financial account details, or other sensitive information through chapter forms or the website guide.

Your choices

  • Email updates: use the unsubscribe link in a chapter newsletter, or email us for help.
  • Corrections and deletion: contact us about information you have provided or content we publish. We will review the request in light of the records involved and any retention requirements.
  • External services: manage your account, group membership, and privacy settings directly with the relevant provider.
  • Browser data: use your browser controls to clear this site’s cookies and stored information.

We may update this policy as the website or chapter practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
