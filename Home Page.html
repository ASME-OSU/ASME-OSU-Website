Who We Are

The Ohio State University ASME student chapter is one of the largest and most active engineering organizations on campus. We connect mechanical engineering students with industry professionals, cutting-edge research, and a community of passionate engineers.

Whether you're looking for internship connections, hands-on project experience, or just great people to learn with, ASME has something for you.
