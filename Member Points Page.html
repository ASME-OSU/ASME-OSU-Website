Planned Features

What the Points System Will Track

Members will earn points by showing up, getting involved, and contributing to the chapter. Points roll up into a semester leaderboard and unlock recognition rewards.

📅

Events Attended

Earn points for each GBM, info session, workshop, and social you attend.

🙋

Volunteer Hours

Log hours helping set up events, running workshops, or supporting chapter operations.

🏆

Leaderboard

Live semester rankings updated after each event so members can see where they stand.

🎁

Rewards

Top members earn recognition, ASME merch, and priority access to exclusive events.

📊

Point Categories

Different event types carry different point values — professional events weighted higher than socials.

🔍

Personal Dashboard

See your own point history, attended events, and semester total in one place.

How Points Work

1️⃣

Attend Events

Show up to GBMs, info sessions, workshops, and socials. Check-in will be tracked at the door.

2️⃣

Points are Logged

The board records attendance after each event. Your total updates automatically on the leaderboard.

3️⃣

Earn Recognition

Top members at the end of the semester are recognized at the final GBM and eligible for rewards.
