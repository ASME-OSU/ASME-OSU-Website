Mission Statement

Helping students grow as engineers and professionals.

ASME OSU is The Ohio State University Student Section of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers. Our mission is to promote science and engineering while giving our members opportunities to engage in industry networking, strengthen technical and professional skills, and build industry contacts.

We accomplish this mission through corporate-sponsored meetings, information sessions, community outreach, social events, and other chapter activities.

01

Industry

Company info sessions, networking events, and direct connections to recruiters at top engineering firms.

02

Community

Social events, outreach, and a welcoming engineering student network built on collaboration.

03

Growth

Leadership roles, technical workshops, resume reviews, and career-focused opportunities.

Recognition

2026 ASME Student Section Achievement Award

OSU ASME was recognized as one of the top-performing student chapters in the United States by the American Society of Mechanical Engineers national organization. This award is given annually to chapters that demonstrate outstanding performance in programming, membership engagement, industry partnerships, and service to the engineering community.

Our History

Part of a Tradition Since 1880

The Ohio State University ASME student section is one of hundreds of chapters affiliated with the American Society of Mechanical Engineers, the professional organization founded in 1880 that sets the global standard for mechanical engineering practice. OSU's chapter has maintained active programming and board leadership continuously, building a record of engagement that earned national recognition in 2026.
