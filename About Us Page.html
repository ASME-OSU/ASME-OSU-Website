What Members Do

Use ASME in the way that helps you most.

Come to one event or help shape the chapter. There is a useful next step whether you are exploring engineering, preparing for recruiting, or ready to lead.

01

Meet Employers

Hear directly from engineers and recruiters through company sessions, technical talks, and networking events.

02

Build Practical Skills

Practice career skills, learn beyond the classroom, and take part in workshops, projects, and competitions.

03

Find Your Community

Meet students across class years through socials, outreach, service, and chapter collaborations.

04

Lead and Serve

Volunteer, organize events, support other members, and pursue committee or executive-board roles.

What a Semester Looks Like

A simple path from first event to real involvement.

There is no single required path. This is how many members turn a first visit into useful relationships, experience, and leadership.

01

Discover

Join the mailing list and choose an event that matches your interests.

02

Connect

Meet members, officers, engineers, and recruiters in a low-pressure setting.

03

Participate

Return for a workshop, social, project, outreach activity, or company session.

04

Contribute

Volunteer, share an idea, help run an event, or explore a leadership role.
